Engine Architecture & Pedagogical Foundations

Traditional educational platforms either show static question sequences or rely on simple random selection. This leaves lagging students frustrated and advanced students unengaged. EduAdaptive solves this by turning real-time predictive ML into an intelligent tutor.

1️⃣

Mastery Classifier (Supervised ML)

Trained on student historical logs to predict \(P(\text{correct})\). Uses Logistic Regression with features:

  • Topic Accuracy: Laplace-smoothed empirical correctness rate.
  • Directional Streak: Momentum tracker (-3 to +3).
  • Difficulty Rating: Cognitive demand (1 to 5).
  • Practice Volume: Log-scaled cumulative repetitions.
2️⃣

Zone of Proximal Development (ZPD)

Based on educational psychologist Lev Vygotsky’s research, optimal learning occurs when tasks are challenging yet achievable.

  • Target Probability Window: Recommends questions with \(P(\text{success}) \approx 0.60 - 0.75\).
  • Frustration Avoidance: Penalizes items with \(P < 0.35\).
  • Boredom Avoidance: Penalizes items with \(P > 0.90\).
3️⃣

Dynamic Online Mastery Update

When a question is answered, the system executes an immediate Bayesian residual update:

ΔM = η · (actual - predicted) · (diff / 5)

Solving a Level 5 problem yields significant mastery growth; failing a Level 1 problem adjusts readiness down to rebuild foundations.
